# XRDICA

A daily cipher puzzle game by Pehr Jansson.

Play at: https://xrdica.com

---

## What Is XRDICA?

XRDICA is a **cryptogram** — the classic "crack the substitution cipher" puzzle newspapers have run for decades (Cryptoquote, Cryptoquip). Every letter in the puzzle has been swapped for a cipher; the same letter always gets the same cipher. Your job is to figure out which cipher is which letter.

---

## How to Play

Each tile shows a cipher instead of a letter. Click a tile and type your guess — it fills every tile sharing that cipher across the whole grid. The same puzzle may include multiple rows (words, a poem, a quote — whatever the day's puzzle is).

- **Click a tile**, then **type a letter** to guess it
- **Arrow keys** move between tiles
- **Backspace / Delete** clears a guess and steps back
- **Spacebar** clears the current tile — unless the puzzle hides spaces too (you'll see a note above the grid), in which case spacebar guesses a space instead
- Selecting a tile highlights every other tile with the same cipher, in orange

Press **GUESS** (or Enter) to check everything at once:
- **Green** = correct, locked in for good
- **Red** = wrong — clears on your next move

**Scoring** — lower is better:
- +1 per keystroke or backspace
- +1 every 5 seconds
- −1 per correct letter on GUESS
- +2 per incorrect cipher on GUESS

Your final score shows the breakdown, e.g. `345 (100 time based and 245 guess based)` — so you can see whether speed or accuracy drove your result.

**Daily Puzzle** — new each day (at midnight, your local time), same for everyone. If nothing's been posted yet for the day, an auto-generated puzzle plays instead — there's always something to solve.

**Random Game** — click ⚄ Random for a random puzzle, or enter a puzzle number to challenge a friend to the exact same one.

**Archive** — browse past days on a calendar. The **Back to Daily** button (or clicking the **XRDICA** title) always brings you back to today's puzzle.

**Pause** — click ⏸ to stop the clock. The game also auto-pauses if you switch tabs, windows, or apps, so you're never penalized for stepping away.
