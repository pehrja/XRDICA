Czech word list (wordlist.txt)

Derived from "FrequencyWords" by Hermit Dave
(https://github.com/hermitdave/FrequencyWords), the Czech OpenSubtitles 2018
frequency list, which is licensed CC BY-SA 4.0
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).

Adapted for XRDICA: restricted to words of 5-10 letters; checked against a
spelling dictionary to remove names, foreign words and typos (Czech standard-language Hunspell dictionary (Miroslav Pošta et al.), GPL -
used only as a filter, not redistributed); vulgar and offensive terms removed;
limited to the 20,000 most frequent remaining words.

This adapted list is shared under the same license, CC BY-SA 4.0.
