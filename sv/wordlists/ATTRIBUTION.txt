Swedish word list (wordlist.txt)

Derived from "FrequencyWords" by Hermit Dave
(https://github.com/hermitdave/FrequencyWords), the Swedish OpenSubtitles 2018
frequency list, which is licensed CC BY-SA 4.0
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).

Adapted for XRDICA: restricted to words of 5-10 letters in a-z/å/ä/ö; checked
against a Swedish spelling dictionary (Göran Andersson's Swedish dictionary for
Hunspell, LGPL 3.0 — used only as a filter, not redistributed) to remove names,
foreign words and typos; vulgar and offensive terms removed; limited to the
20,000 most frequent remaining words.

This adapted list is shared under the same license, CC BY-SA 4.0.
